## Evidence handling

- Facts derived from a source remain separate from Market Squawk-specific design inferences.
- Repository behavior and platform rules are date-sensitive and require refresh at the frozen release
  candidate.
- Historical specifications remain useful for threat-model or research context but do not override
  current official platform documentation.
- A selected project is pattern evidence, not an authorization to copy its code, license model,
  dependency choices, trust shortcuts, or incomplete lifecycle behavior.
